Select the release tag matching this extension's version, v<VERSION>, then
download texpresso-<VERSION>-sources-<target>.tar.gz and its SHA-256 checksum.
Targets are darwin-arm64, darwin-x64, linux-x64, linux-arm64 and universal.

applicable patches/recipes, original notices, and build/replacement instructions.
